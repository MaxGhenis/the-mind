The Mind: Timing Experiment

The Mind is a card game where players must play cards 1-100 in order, using only their sense of timing.


In this experiment, you'll see a card value and:


1. Predict: How many seconds would you wait before playing?

2. React: Watch time pass, click when you'd actually play.


We're measuring if your prediction matches your reaction.

10 trials, ~3 minutes total
